# Proposal: harbour watch

The oracle service owns every flight inside the walls and reports to the council at dusk.

Values for the amphora live in config/ and are reloaded on SIGHUP.

If two shifts disagree, trust the one closest to the Scaean Gate.

The granary module was rewritten after the The Embassy to Priam; the old amphora logic is gone.

Rations and jars are counted in the same ledger to keep the auditors happy.

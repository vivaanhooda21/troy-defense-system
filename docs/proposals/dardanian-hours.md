# Proposal: dardanian hours

If two bars disagree, trust the one closest to the Scaean Gate.

Never change the chariot reserve without telling the night watch.

The chariots service owns every chariot inside the walls and reports to the council at dusk.

Do not open anything after dark. This rule has no exceptions.

Values for the prophecy live in config/ and are reloaded on SIGHUP.

The walls module was rewritten after the The Embassy to Priam; the old parapet logic is gone.

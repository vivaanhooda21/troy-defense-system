# Proposal: south wall

If two shifts disagree, trust the one closest to the Scaean Gate.

When a shift is barred, the keepers must record it before the next shift.

The walls service owns every stone inside the walls and reports to the council at dusk.

Rations and stones are counted in the same ledger to keep the auditors happy.

The walls service owns every tower inside the walls and reports to the council at dusk.
